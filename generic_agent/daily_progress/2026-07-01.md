## 判明した重要 RAM/ROM (US Emerald)

- gBattleTypeFlags = 0x02022FEC (whiteout で stale、CB2 で gate 要)
- gMain.callback2 = 0x030022C4 (overworld=0x08085E5D / battle=0x08038421)
- gBattleMons = 0x02024084 (0x58 bytes; [0]=自分 [1]=敵; moves@0x0C type@0x21/0x22 hp@0x28)
- gBattleMoves ROM = 0x0831C898 (12 bytes/move; power@+1 type@+2)
- badge flags = event flag 0x867..0x86E
